# 🍁🌕 Moon Learning Guide: Canadian Space Agency (CSA) Resources
*The Moon and "living on the Moon" parts of the CSA's STEM and youth resources, picked for a kids' Moon-base game and lessons*

> **What this source is.** The page you gave is an **educator directory** (links to tools, activities, games and services). It has no Moon data table. So I opened its Moon-related pages (the **Activities and experiments** list and **Indigenous ways of knowing**) and kept only what relates to the Moon and to living on it. All descriptions below are my own summaries. The CSA's materials are **copyrighted**, so check their Notices and copyright page before reusing any of them.

## 2. 🌙 Moon activities (all marked "Moon" by the CSA)

*"Grades" are the levels listed by the CSA, not ages.*

| Activity | Grades | What kids do | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Creating Moon craters** | 3–6 | Drop objects to see how mass, speed, angle and height change crater size | 30 min |
| **My Moon** | 3–9 | Search-and-find on the Moon's surface, phases of the Moon, Apollo missions | 40–60 min |
| **Draw a comic strip: history of the Moon** | 3–9 | Draw how the Moon formed and changed | 30–45 min |
| **Move like the Earth and the Moon** | 1–6 | Act out Earth, Moon and Sun motion with friends | 40 min |
| **Learning about the Moon** | 5 and up | Explore the Moon, phases and eclipses with the free WorldWide Telescope | Varies |
| **Moons in the Solar System card game** | 2 and up | Printable card game about moons | Varies |
| **Mission: Astronaut** (digital game) | 3–6 | Missions and mini-games on astronaut training, living in space, the Moon, health and nutrition | Varies |
| **Reach for the Top / Who will reach the top? / Can you find all the answers? / The Moon in questions / Cast your vote!** | 4 and up | Team quiz and voting games about the Moon | Varies |
| **Make a pollinator** | K–2 | Build a plant-pollination system for space | 60 min |
| **A simple pollination system** | 3–5 | Same idea using a wheel-and-axle | 60 min |
| **Pollinating pulleys** | 6–8 | Pulley-based pollination system for Gateway (lunar orbit) | 60 min |

## 3. 🏠 Living on the Moon: surviving, working, eating

| Activity | Grades | Moon-base skill it teaches | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Survivor: Moon** | 6–9 | Rank the items a crew needs to get back to the lunar base | 90 min |
| **Lunar Resupply Mission** | 6–9 | Design, budget, build and test a lander that delivers supplies | 2.5 hrs |
| **Lunar Rover Research Challenge** | 6–9 | Design a lunar rover research mission. *(Competition years listed were 2022–2023, so check if it is still running)* | Varies |
| **Are You Ready to Rock?** | 6–9 | Crew teamwork collecting rock samples on the surface | 20–25 min |
| **Lunar Rescue** | 6–9 | Rescue a crewmember on the lunar surface | 25–30 min |
| **Interplanetary Farming** | 6–9 | Which crops suit the ISS, Moon and Mars | 100 min |
| **Space farmer (mini-greenhouse)** | 4 and up | Sprout and monitor seeds | 30 min |
| **Space gardener: plant roots** | 6–12 | How soil nutrients change root size and shape (links to Moon-soil research) | Days |
| **Tomatosphere™** | K–12 | Seed germination after "space" exposure | Days |
| **Future of food** | K–12 | Food production in space and on Earth, agri-food careers | Varies |
| **Living Space** | 6–9 | Environmental monitoring for health, coding and data | 4–8 classes |
| **Space Brain Hack** | 6–12 | Ideas for astronaut mental health and food production | Varies |
| **Build a Spacesuit** | 4–9 | Make parts like an astronaut suit | 45–60 min |
| **First Steps: Training for Space** | 6–9 | Fitness test based on a real astronaut recruitment test | 30–45 min |
| **Moving and working in space** | 6–12 | Tethers and manual tasks on spacewalks | 2 hrs |
| **Space Repairs** | 6–9 | Crew teamwork fixing broken equipment | 30 min |
| **Mars Base: Now Accepting Applications** | 6–9 | Prove your job is critical to a new base *(Mars, but the role idea fits the Moon base)* | 90 min |
| **Dragons' Den** | 6–9 | Pitch an invention for space exploration | 90 min |
| **Patch Maker** | 6–9 | Design an individual and a crew mission patch | 90 min |

## 4. 🪶 Indigenous Moon knowledge

| Resource | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Indigenous Moon stories and the 13 Moons cycle** | Written by researcher and Knowledge Keeper **Wilfred Buck** |
| **Star Scribe** (game) | Players visit the surface of the Moon and learn **Cree constellations** from Elder and Star Lore teacher Wilfred Buck, then trace the constellations and reveal artwork |
| **Root & STEM: Skywatchers** | Free print and online STEAM issue (Pinnguaq Association, supported by CSA) with articles, podcasts, games, comics and lesson plans |
| **Jeremy Hansen's Artemis II patch** | Patch created with Anishinaabe artist Henry Guimond and Dave Courchene III (Turtle Lodge, Sagkeeng First Nation) to recognize Indigenous Peoples |
| **Nunavut food blog** | "Nunavut youth learn about growing food at home and in space" |

## 7. Cautions
- This guide covers the **titles and short descriptions** of the CSA activities. I did not open each activity page, so check supplies and steps there.
- Grade levels are the CSA's, and a "difficulty" scale is listed on their page. Match them to your players.
- Some programs may have ended (e.g. the Lunar Rover Research Challenge competition dates).
- Cultural material such as Indigenous stories belongs to its knowledge keepers. Use it as the CSA presents it, with credit, and don't change it.
